## Responses
- **Write one feature from the map that, if an engineer asked “why is this a priority for our 12-week roadmap?”, you honestly could not give a strategic answer for.:** Feature: “See balance”

I could not give a strong strategic answer for this one in a 12-week roadmap. The map does not show how it improves collection outcomes or reduces agent contact. It is more of a hygiene feature: customers expect it, and it probably already exists in your app or portal. The only honest argument is “the plan journey needs it as a starting point”. That is a dependency, not a priority.

What would change my mind is data. If many agent calls are only “How much do I owe?”, then it becomes a clear contact-reduction feature. Without that number, an engineer could fairly say it can wait or reuse an existing component.
- **Write one feature that looks “correct” for the app but has zero strategic weight, you have no idea if it helps the company make money or whether a single user actually asked for it.:** My pick is the plan simulator with live preview. It looks right for a modern self-service app, because sliders and instant feedback feel helpful. But I added it to the mind map from general product habits, not from evidence. I don’t know if:

a single customer wants to play with plan options, or if they just want a fair offer they can accept
it raises the number of completed plans or reduces defaults
it saves money, because it adds design, build and maintenance cost

Customers in debt are often stressed and want a quick answer. A simulator could even add choice overload and slow them down. To give the feature real weight, test it first: compare a simple “here are 2 offers” screen with a simulator in a prototype, and measure plan completion and agent contact.
